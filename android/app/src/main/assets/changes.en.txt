Stroik Android 1.02

Fixed a crash while restoring the screen, including theme changes, rotation and returning to the app. Android no longer restores checkbox state into a dropdown with a reused view ID. The current tab, scroll position and last result are retained. Restart the microphone after an interruption.

Added recreation tests for every tab in both languages, theme changes and legacy widget state. The signing key is unchanged; Android confirms installation. The Play Protect warning observed for 1.01 is documented without assuming its cause.
